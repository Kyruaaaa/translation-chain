# Scanning rules

After the choreography, the rock is recorded again, this time with a real 3D scanner.

## Before scanning

1. Leave the rock on the turntable for 1 hour to dry.
2. Don't clean it. Slurry and dust stay.
3. Photograph the flake tray from above.

## The scan

1. Stand the rock on its base, the way it sat on the seat.
2. Scan the top and all the way around, with colour texture on.
3. **Don't scan the underside.** The open bottom is what makes the scan a hollow shell.

## Cleaning up the scan

- Remove only what isn't rock, such as the table or the seat.
- Don't fill the bottom or close the mesh. The petals need the open edge.
- Don't fill or smooth anything else. Chips and scratches are the point.
- Keep the rock in one piece.

## Name

Use the rock's ID, plus `_after`.

## Output

A textured hollow shell, which goes to Module. The scan and the flakes together are the rock's record of its day.

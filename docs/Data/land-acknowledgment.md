# Land acknowledgment

This work is made in Providence, Rhode Island, on the homelands of the Narragansett people, and of the Wampanoag and Pokanoket peoples of this region.

These peoples are still here. The land was not empty before it was measured, and it isn't empty now.

This project puts sensors on a building and rocks in the weather, and it treats a forecast as something to compare against. The numbers it collects are a very short record of a place with a much longer one. Nothing here claims to know this land through its readings.

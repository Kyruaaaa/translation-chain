# Land acknowledgment

This work is made in Providence, Rhode Island.

When the rocks were formed, there were no names, no borders, no maps. Only land and water.

The land was not empty before it was measured, and it is not empty now. It was full of blooms. Now it is also full of numbers.

Numbers hold still. They stay inside the lines that rules draw. Blooms do not.

And yet the land no longer blooms without rules.

The land is conscious and sentient.

Here, a rock sits in the weather, and its numbers are held against a forecast. Their record is short. The land's is much longer. These numbers do not claim to know it.

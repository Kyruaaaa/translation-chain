# Placement rules

## Where

- The sensors and the rock sit on a balcony in downtown Providence.
- The wall faces **east**, so the rock gets morning sun and then shade.
- The forecast is asked for this same spot, on a vertical east-facing surface, so both sides describe the same wall.

## The rock

1. Photograph the rock on white **before** it goes outside (see Reconstruction).
2. Mark which side faces the window. This side is the rock's **front**.
3. Put the rock in the harness, with the temperature probe touching its surface.
4. Write down the time it went in. Every reading after that time belongs to this rock.
5. Leave it for one day, 12 am to 11 pm. Nothing touches it.

## The sensors

- Keep the light and UV sensors facing out, with nothing covering them.
- Keep the air sensor in moving air, away from the wall and the board. A warm wall makes the rock seem warmer than it is.
- Power comes from a wall plug. The SD card keeps every reading even if the WiFi drops.

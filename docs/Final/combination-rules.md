# Final combination rules

Two translations of the same days meet.

1. The data patterns join into one cloth: a picture of five weeks of passing time. This is the ground.
2. The blooming rocks are quilted on top as veils. They bloom over the data, but the organza lets the data seep through.

The petals carry what happened to the rock. The embroidery carries what happened in the numbers. Where the flower does not bloom, the data speaks in its place.

## The days

- 35 days, Oct 25 to Nov 28: five weeks.
- Laid out 7 across and 5 down, one week per row, starting on Sunday.

## Order

1. Print each day's petal flower onto an 11 × 11 in piece of organza with dye sublimation.
2. Embroider each day's data as a 12 × 12 in square on one whole piece of linen, 84 × 60 in.
3. Lay each flower on its day's square.
4. Quilt the layers together. The sewing follows the sequence of the movements.

## Alignment

- Centre each 11 in flower on its 12 in square. A ½ in edge of data shows around it.
- Midnight points to the top of the cloth.

## The wall

- It stands next to the quilt.
- Its total length equals the quilt's perimeter: 288 in (24 ft).
- Its shape comes from the shapes of the rocks, and it holds all these days.

## Materials

- Organza, polyester
- Linen
- Embroidery thread
- Quilting thread

## To decide

- How the 12 in squares fit the embroidery machine's hoop.

# Final combination rules

Two translations of one day meet on one piece of fabric. The petals carry what happened to the rock. The embroidery carries what happened in the numbers.

## Order

1. Print the petal flower PNG onto polyester with dye sublimation.
2. Embroider the Bloom pattern on top.

The print comes first, because the heat press would flatten the thread.

## Alignment

- The centre of the Bloom sits on the centre of the flower's core.
- Midnight on the Bloom points to the top of the fabric.
- Place the hoop using the centre point and one mark at midnight.

## Materials

- Polyester, or a polyester blend. Sublimation needs it.
- One thread per measurement, plus one for the forecast.

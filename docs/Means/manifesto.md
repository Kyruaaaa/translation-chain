# Manifesto

A forecast is a promise made to everywhere at once.

A rock keeps no promises. It sits in one place and has one day, the day that actually happened to it.

We measure the gap between them. Not to correct the forecast, and not to correct the rock, but because the gap is the only thing in this project that neither of them made alone.

**Every translation loses something.** A rock becomes numbers. Numbers become movements. Movements become marks. Marks become a scan, a scan becomes a flower, a flower becomes cloth. Nothing arrives whole. What changes along the way is the work.

**The rules come first.** The data decides where, how many, and how hard. The hand only carries it out. Two people following the same day would make the same movements, and still leave different marks, because the stone answers back.

**The attempt will fail.** You can't cool a rock back to a forecast with a few drops of water. You try anyway, for 24 minutes. What the attempt leaves on the stone is the record.

**Nothing is added.** Water evaporates. Everything else only takes stone away.

**The rock decides the line.** Water runs where it runs. Flakes break where they break. Cuts follow the cracks that are already there.

**Strange days stay strange.** No reading is smoothed to look better. No gap is filled in. A missing hour is a pause.

A rock cannot speak. It can only be measured, and we should be honest that the measuring is ours. So this work does not speak for the rock. It only shows, as carefully as it can, how far our picture of a day was from the day itself.

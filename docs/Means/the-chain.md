# The chain

A rock sits outside for one day. A forecast says what that day should have been. The two never match. This project turns that difference into things you can touch.

## 0. Make a copy of the rock

Before anything happens, take six photos of the rock and rebuild it on the computer. This is what the rock looked like before its day.

## 1. Compare

The rock sits on a balcony with sensors beside it. Every 15 minutes, from 12 am to 11 pm, they record the temperature, humidity, light and UV. The last hour is for collecting the SD card and checking that everything is OK. The forecast gives its numbers for the same moments. Then you subtract one from the other.

## 2. Turn the difference into a pattern

Draw the rock's day and the forecast's day as two lines on one graph. The space between the lines is a shape. Those shapes become an embroidery pattern.

## 3. Turn the difference into movements

Every number tells you what to do to the rock, using water, sandpaper, a chisel and a hammer. You try to push the rock back toward what the forecast said. One day takes 24 minutes to perform.

## 4. Scan the rock

The rock now has marks on it. Scan it with a 3D scanner, leaving the bottom open.

## 5. Peel it

Cut the scan open like an orange peel, following its cracks, and lay it flat. It becomes a flower.

## 6. Bring it together

Print the flower onto fabric. Embroider the pattern on top. One day of weather, one rock, one piece of cloth.

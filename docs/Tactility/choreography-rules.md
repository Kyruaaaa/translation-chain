# Choreography rules

The whole piece is one gesture repeated: trying to bring the rock back to what the forecast said. You never fully succeed. What the attempt leaves on the stone is the record.

Nothing is added to the rock. The water evaporates, and everything else only takes stone away.

## Time

- 24 minutes. Minute *h* performs hour *h* of the rock's day.
- Minute 23 has no data, because the last hour is for checking. Pause for the whole minute.
- Each minute has four 15-second beats: temperature, humidity, light, UV.
- In a beat: 0–2 s pick up the tool, 2–12 s act, 12–15 s put it down.
- If the action isn't finished at 12 s, stop anyway.

## Setup

- A turntable with 24 marks, and a fixed pointer that stands for the window.
- The side that faced the window goes toward mark 06. Crown up.
- At the start of each minute, turn the table one mark. The slice at the pointer is **the face**.
- The face has three bands: crown, middle, foot.

## Level

Each beat's level comes from that hour's average gap.

| Level | Temperature | Humidity | Light | UV | Force |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | under 0.5 °C | under 3 % | under 8 % | under 0.3 | Pause |
| 1 | 0.5–1.5 °C | 3–9 % | 8–24 % | 0.3–0.9 | Wrist |
| 2 | 1.5–3 °C | 9–18 % | 24–48 % | 0.9–1.8 | Elbow |
| 3 | over 3 °C | over 18 % | over 48 % | over 1.8 | Shoulder |

**Pause:** both palms flat on the face, still, for 15 s.

## Temperature: water

- Warmer than forecast: cold water, 5 °C. Colder: warm water, 40 °C.
- Place: top of the crown band, on the centre line.

| Level | Drops | Height |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 5 cm |
| 2 | 2 | 10 cm |
| 3 | 4 | 20 cm |

Then score one line per drop along the trail, with the point chisel held like a pen. Follow the water. Never wipe it.

## Humidity: sandpaper

- Wetter than forecast: P80, dry, horizontal strokes. Blow the dust away.
- Drier: P400, wet, vertical strokes. Leave the slurry.
- Place: a square in the middle band.

| Level | Patch | Strokes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 × 2 cm | 4 |
| 2 | 3 × 3 cm | 8 |
| 3 | 4 × 4 cm | 16 |

## Light: chisel

- Place: foot band.
- Brighter than forecast: peck with the point chisel at 90°.
- Darker: cut a flat facet with the flat chisel at 20°.

| Level | Peck | Facet |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 taps, 1 cm circle | 3 blows, 1 × 1 cm |
| 2 | 10 taps, 1.5 cm circle | 5 blows, 1.5 × 1.5 cm |
| 3 | 20 taps, 2 cm circle | 8 blows, 2 × 2 cm |

## UV: hammer

- More sun than forecast: work on the face.
- Less sun: work on the opposite slice, *h* + 12.
- Place: the crown edge. Flat chisel 5 mm in, angled 45° outward.
- Blows: 1, 2 or 3, by level. Move 5 mm clockwise after each.
- A blow that breaks nothing still counts. Don't repeat it.
- Every flake goes in the tray, in compartment *h*.

## If something goes wrong

| If | Then |
|---|---|
| A reading is missing | Pause, and note it |
| The patch is bigger than the face | Use the largest square that fits |
| The rock splits | Keep the piece with most of the face on the table. The other goes in the tray. |
| You lose your place | Hands down. Rejoin at the next tone. Never go back. |

## After

Leave the rock on the turntable for 1 hour to dry.

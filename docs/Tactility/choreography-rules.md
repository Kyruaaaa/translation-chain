# Choreography rules

**Pattern 04: Tactility**
One rock. One day. Four pieces. Sewn by hand, in sound.

The whole piece is one gesture repeated: trying to bring the rock back to what the forecast said. You never fully succeed. What the attempt leaves on the stone is the record. Nothing is added to the rock. The water evaporates, and everything else only takes stone away.

---

## Envelope

| | |
|---|---|
| Finished length | 21 min 40 s |
| Pieces | 4, sewn one after another |
| Each piece | 5 min 25 s, the whole day from 12 am to 11 pm |
| Each hour | about 13½ s |
| Seam allowance | the last 13½ s of each piece is silent: the checking hour. Breathe, and change tools. |

## Notions

- Cold water (5 °C) and warm water (40 °C), with droppers
- Point chisel
- Sandpaper, P80 and P400
- Flat chisel
- Hammer
- A speaker, playing the day's sound, and its cue sheet

## Grainline

The **front** of the rock is the side that faced the window. Every piece is worked on the front, except where the pattern says **back**.

## Sizes

The pulse's speed is its size. The bigger the gap between rock and forecast, the quicker the pulse, and the bigger the work.

| Size | Sound | Force | Temperature | Humidity | Light | UV |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | hum | **Rest**: both palms flat on the rock | under 0.5 °C | under 3 % | under 8 % | under 0.3 |
| 1 | pulse every 3 s | Wrist | 0.5–1.5 °C | 3–9 % | 8–24 % | 0.3–0.9 |
| 2 | pulse every 2 s | Elbow | 1.5–3 °C | 9–18 % | 24–48 % | 0.9–1.8 |
| 3 | pulse every 1½ s | Shoulder | over 3 °C | over 18 % | over 48 % | over 1.8 |

## Markings

| Mark | Means |
|---|---|
| ↑ | Rising cue: the rock is **more** than the forecast |
| ↓ | Falling cue: the rock is **less** than the forecast |
| ○ | Circles |
| — | Horizontal lines |
| ǀ | Vertical lines |
| · · · | The pulse: one pulse, one movement |

## How to read the sound

The sound is soft and electronic, only gentle tones. Each piece has its own note: temperature a middle tone, humidity a lower tone, light a higher tone, UV a low tone.

- **Cue:** three soft chimes, only when something **changes**: the size changes, or the rock flips between more and less. Rising chimes ↑, falling chimes ↓, three equal chimes for size 0.
- **Pulse:** after the cue, a slow pulse of soft swells guides you until the next cue. **One pulse, one movement.** Each swell leans up ↑ or down ↓, like its cue.
- **Speed:** size 1 is a pulse every 3 s, size 2 every 2 s, size 3 every 1½ s. Bigger is a little louder.
- **Hum:** a soft, steady hum means size 0. Hold still, palms flat.
- **Silence:** only in the checking hour, at the end of each piece.
- A day gives about 2 to 11 cues per piece.

---

## Piece A: Temperature

**Sound:** a middle tone, with a soft high chime for each drop.

1. ↑ Drip **cold** water. ↓ Drip **warm** water.
2. Drip at the top of the rock, one drop for each soft high chime. Size 1: 1 drop. Size 2: 2 drops. Size 3: 4 drops.
3. · · · Then light, even taps. Score along the water's trail with the point chisel, held like a pen, one line per tap. Follow the water. Never wipe it.

## Piece B: Humidity

**Sound:** a lower tone.

1. ↑ **P80**, dry, in ○ circles. Blow the dust away.
2. ↓ **P400**, wet, in — lines. Leave the slurry.
3. · · · One stroke per pulse, on the same patch. Size 1 is small and light, size 3 is wide and heavy.

## Piece C: Light

**Sound:** a higher tone.

1. ↑ Brighter: ǀ vertical marks.
2. ↓ Darker: — horizontal marks.
3. · · · One mark per pulse. Size 1 is small and shallow, size 3 is long and deep.

## Piece D: UV

**Sound:** a low tone, with one low swell for each blow.

1. ↑ More sun: hammer the **front**.
2. ↓ Less sun: hammer the **back**.
3. One blow for each low swell. Size 1: 1 blow. Size 2: 2 blows. Size 3: 3 blows. A blow that breaks nothing still counts.
4. Then the hum: hold still. Every flake goes in the tray, by hour.

---

## Mending

| If | Then |
|---|---|
| A reading is missing | The hum. Rest, and note it. |
| The rock splits | Keep the larger piece. The other goes in the tray. |
| You lose your place | Hands down. Rejoin at the next pulse. Never go back. |

## Finishing

Leave the rock to dry for 1 hour.
